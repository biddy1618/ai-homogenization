How it's measured

Every week the tracker asks Google the same 100 everyday questions, twenty each in finance, health, law, science and tech. It reads the first five pages that answer each one and compares what they say, by meaning rather than exact wording.

The score runs from 0, where the five pages share nothing, to 1, where they say the same thing. The line is the average across the questions readable that week. The first 45 questions have been tracked since 04 Oct; the panel grew to 100 on 26 Oct. Movement week to week is still small, and the series is young.
