How it's measured

Every week the tracker asks Google the same everyday questions in health, finance, law, science and tech. For each one it reads the first five pages that can be read and compares what they say by meaning, not exact wording. This week 93 of the 100 questions could be read.

The score runs from 0, where the five pages share nothing, to 1, where they say the same thing. The line is the average over the questions read in each run. The panel grew from 45 to 100 questions on 26 October 2026; runs before that read the original 45. Questions are never edited or removed.

The carved bust and the cast figures are scale anchors, not data: low on the scale means many distinct voices, high means one face repeated. Movement from week to week is still small and the series is young, so read the line as a level, not a trend.
