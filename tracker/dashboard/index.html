Homogenization Tracker

Every Monday we take Google's top results for a fixed panel of informational questions in five domains, read the first 5 pages that can be read in full, and measure how alike they are. Similarity is the average cosine similarity between the pages' text embeddings — the same measure as the accompanying study of Stack Exchange and research abstracts (higher = the top pages say more of the same). Distinct pages out of 5 (Vendi score) counts how many genuinely different pages there are: 5 = all different, 1 = all the same.
