Subject: AI "sameness" — it holds up outside Stack Exchange (arXiv, PubMed, Hacker News)

Hey Mark,

Quick follow-up from this morning. I took the same method we used on the Stack Exchange
communities and ran it on three totally different platforms: arXiv (CS papers), PubMed (cancer
abstracts), and Hacker News (tech-forum comments).

The gist: the ChatGPT-era rise in "sameness" shows up on all three. After ChatGPT (late 2022),
answers/abstracts became measurably more similar to each other, and it holds up even after we
control for answers just getting longer.

A few notes:

- Hacker News is the cleanest - flat for years, then a clear turn upward right after ChatGPT.
- arXiv and PubMed were already slowly converging beforehand, and ChatGPT looks like it sped that
  up rather than starting it (so read those two as "acceleration," not a clean break).
- Same honest caveat as before: this is the overall/aggregate rise. I haven't yet re-run the
  "topic" control on these three, so I can't yet say whether it's people writing in the same
  *style* vs. writing about the same *topics*. That's the next step.

Attached: two charts per source (a similarity trend and a before/after slope version), plus a
one-page highlights file that explains how to read them.

Happy to walk through it whenever.
