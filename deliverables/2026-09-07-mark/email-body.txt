Subject: Quick update on the AI "sameness" question — with charts

Hey Mark,

Picking up from our call. I've now run the analysis across 26 different online Q&A communities
(~1 million answers, through mid-2026). Short version first, charts if you want to dig in.

The gist: I'm still not seeing good evidence that AI made people write the same way. Answers do get
a little more similar to each other after ChatGPT in most communities - but once I account for the fact
that people are simply writing about different stuff now, that bump mostly disappears. So it looks like
"what people write about" changed, not "how people write."

A few things worth knowing:

- Out of 26 communities, 18 show a real (statistically significant) uptick in similarity. That means
  8 do NOT - a bit more than the ~5 I eyeballed on the call. Cooking (Seasoned Advice) actually drifts
  slightly down.

- The "high brainpower" communities (stats, law, physics...) and the "everyday" ones (cooking, travel,
  gaming...) move basically the same. So the idea that AI flattens the easy stuff the most doesn't hold up.
